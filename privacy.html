Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Shorts Sync is a personal tool used only by its developer with their own accounts.

What data is used

With the owner's authorization via TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and a refresh token for the owner's TikTok account. The token is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts. The app does not read, collect or store any other TikTok data or data about other people.

Where data is stored

Tokens are kept in a private GitHub repository and GitHub Actions secrets controlled by the owner. They are not shared with any third party and not used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.
